# Project Card

## Dataset and Subset
- Path C: NACC Alzheimer's Data (UDS + MRI)
- Subset: Pre-defined subset provided by the teaching team.

## Temporary Project Title
Association between Brain Structural Volumes/Thickness and Cognitive Status in the NACC Dataset.

## Main Statistical Question
Are specific structural brain MRI measurements (e.g., Entorhinal Volume or Hippocampal structures) significantly associated with cognitive diagnostic categories (Normal vs. Impaired) from the UDS dataset?

## Unit of Observation and Target Population
- Unit of Observation: A single, independent clinical/MRI visit per unique patient.
- Target Population: Older adults evaluated for cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease at NACC centers.

## Main Variables and Measurement Scales
1. Cognitive Status (NACCUDSD): Categorical (Ordinal/Nominal) - from UDS.
2. Brain Volumes (e.g., LH_ENTORHINAL_GVOL): Quantitative Continuous - from MRI.
3. Brain Thickness (e.g., LH_ENTORHINAL_AVGTH): Quantitative Continuous - from MRI.
4. Demographics (e.g., Age, Sex): Quantitative Continuous / Categorical Nominal - from UDS.

## Role of Variables in the Question
- Explanatory Variable (Independent): MRI Measurements (Volume/Thickness) and Demographics.
- Response Variable (Dependent / Grouping): Cognitive Status (NACCUDSD).

## Type of Question
Primarily Comparative and Associative (Investigating differences in brain structure across cognitive groups).

## Initial Hypothesis
Patients with impaired cognitive status have significantly lower cortical volumes and thickness in temporal/parietal regions (such as the Entorhinal cortex) compared to cognitively normal individuals.
